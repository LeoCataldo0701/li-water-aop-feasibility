**Treatment Options**

Most of Long Island relies on carbon adsorption to treat PFAS contamination but is limited:

- Carbon fails on short-chain PFAS like PFNA 
- Carbon containing contaminants must be landfilled or disposed of separately

Photo-Fenton Advanced Oxidation is a newer approach that has its own sets of advantages and limitations

- Directly breaks down the compounds
- Can be pricey because of iron and UV light costs
- Some radicals have a problem breaking down the C-F bond
